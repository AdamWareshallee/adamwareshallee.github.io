About Me

I'm a Computing and IT graduate who enjoys learning about technology and building things with it. I also love learning in general and consider myself a very curious person. I enjoy exploring different subjects, challenging myself and continuously improving.

I'm also a competitive person who likes to push myself and strive to be the best I can be. When I make mistakes, I try to understand what went wrong and think about what would be the solution to this. I then keep working until I find one. In my experience, this makes finding a solution even more rewarding, especially when programming or configuring the CLI while working with networking.

In my downtime, I enjoy reading books, watching videos and movies, and spending time outdoors. I also make it a habit to go to the park each day to get some fresh air, clear my mind and relax.

Now that I've finished my degree, I want to put what I've learned into practice through my work while continuing to learn new things, challenge myself and experience new adventures. Most importantly, I want to continue growing as a person and be a good person along the way.
